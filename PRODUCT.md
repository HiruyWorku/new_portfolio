# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary visitors are recruiters, engineering leaders, researchers, and potential collaborators evaluating Hiruy Worku's work, technical range, and point of view. This is inferred from the portfolio content and recruiting links supplied in the project.

## Product Purpose

Present Hiruy's software engineering, AI infrastructure, accessibility research, projects, and experience in a form that is fast to explore and memorable without obscuring the substance. Success means a visitor can understand the work, inspect relevant proof, and reach Hiruy without reading one long page.

## Positioning

The portfolio connects production engineering, AI systems, and accessibility research through specific projects and papers rather than broad personal-brand claims.

## Operating Context

Visitors commonly arrive from a resume, GitHub, LinkedIn, or a direct introduction. They may have only a few minutes and need to move quickly between overview, selected work, research, experience, and contact information.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the existing Flask application, responsive web delivery, resume link, external project and research links, and working contact form.
- Organize substantial content into one clearly indexed editorial page with concise sections and native disclosures for secondary detail.
- Keep the personal photograph small or omit it; no badge or portfolio-pass treatment.
- Prefer static presentation and browser-native interactions over authored animation.
- Do not fabricate clients, testimonials, metrics, publications, or commercial claims.

## Brand Commitments

The name is Hiruy Worku. The voice should be direct, intelligent, personal, and professional. The experience must feel original rather than copied from the supplied inspiration. User-supplied anti-pattern constraints are binding: avoid harsh gradients, rainbow or neon color, purple-and-black styling, pure white, drop shadows, liquid glass, bento grids, terminal windows, dot grids, radial decoration, soft pill-like UI, generic three-card rows, and hover-led spectacle.

The durable visual preference is a professional, minimal, content-first personal site in the tradition of well-edited academic and engineering portfolios. Typography, writing, and proof should carry the page. Animation and interface spectacle should not.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains real project descriptions, research links, work history, a profile photograph, company logos, resume and social links, and contact handling. It contains no verified testimonials, performance metrics, or client endorsements; future work must not invent them.

## Product Principles

- Let real work and research lead the experience.
- Make dense information easy to traverse in a few deliberate actions.
- Avoid nonessential motion.
- Keep personal imagery subordinate to the work.
- Favor a recognizable point of view over portfolio-template conventions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic structure, keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts, and readable contrast. Accessibility is also part of Hiruy's research identity and should be reflected in the quality of the interface.
