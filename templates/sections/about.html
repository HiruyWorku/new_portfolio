I got into engineering by repairing appliances and building things from scratch. I still approach problems the same way: figure out how everything works, then build something that helps.

I'm majoring in Computer Science and Mathematics at The College of Wooster. I've built production systems at NVIDIA and Schneider Electric, and I've recently started contributing to open source. For my senior thesis, I built Tandem, a video-calling tool that brings together sign recognition, captions, and speech.

Outside engineering, I try to make time for cinematography, photography, design, drawing, and prototyping. I watch a lot of movies, too.
